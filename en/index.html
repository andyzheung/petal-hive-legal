Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Shop or Settings and choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Consumable coins and tools cannot be restored once spent; the Sakura Courtyard garden and other non-consumables can.

Why is a purchase still pending?

Apple processes all purchases. Wait for the App Store to finish, then reopen the Shop. If a purchase is cancelled or awaiting approval, the game grants nothing until the store confirms it; a completed purchase that was interrupted is delivered again automatically the next time you launch the game.

Where is my progress kept? How do I start over?

Progress, flowers, garden decor, and settings are stored on your device. Removing the app can remove this local data. To start fresh, use Start a fresh garden in Settings.

How is the game language chosen?

The game follows your system language: Chinese systems get Chinese, everything else gets English. Shop prices are shown in your local currency by the App Store.

Why does the Shop say unavailable?

Browsing prices and buying requires an App Store connection. While offline you cannot see prices or purchase, but the core game keeps working and you can keep stacking blooms.
