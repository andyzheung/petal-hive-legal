Petal Hive 蜂巢花开

Privacy Policy

Applies to iOS 1.0 · Updated October 4, 2026

Support Terms of Service 简体中文

In short: Petal Hive has no account and no developer-operated server. Your garden stays on your device. Usage analytics and crash reports are off by default and are only collected after you turn on "Share usage insights" in Settings, and you can turn them off at any time. Version 1.0 shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

1. Data we collect

By default, we collect nothing. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or other identifiers. Version 1.0 contains no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking.

2. Optional usage insights and error reports

You may opt in from Settings with "Share usage insights". When enabled, the app sends anonymous usage events and error reports through Firebase (Google LLC), such as level start and end, achievements unlocked, and purchase outcome categories (never amounts, transaction identifiers, or account details), used to tune difficulty and stability. The feature is off by default; turning it off stops collection.

3. On-device game data

The game stores progress, flowers, garden decor, settings, and purchase entitlements inside the app container on your device so the game works, including fully offline. This data is not sent to developer servers (other than the anonymous events described above when you opt in). Deleting the app may remove this local data. Device backups are governed by your device, Apple Account, and system settings.

4. In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, gardener tool kits, the honey jar, and the paid garden) are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Payment methods, billing information, and your Apple Account are handled by Apple, not PlayCubes. The game receives store transaction results to grant or restore purchased items and never sees your full payment details.

5. Advertising and tracking

Version 1.0 shows no ads, has no rewarded ads, uses no advertising identifier, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data lives on your device; clear it with "Start a fresh garden" in Settings or by deleting the app. Purchase history is retained by Apple and tied to your Apple Account for restoration where applicable. Anonymous events collected after opt-in are handled by Google (Firebase) under its retention policies.

7. Children's privacy

Petal Hive is not designed to collect personal information from children and is made for adult casual players. We collect no data by default. Parents or guardians can contact us by email with any privacy question.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds ads, accounts, or other data practices, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before enabling them.
